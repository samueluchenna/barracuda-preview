01

Getting in

Where is the gate?

Three steps from the main road, and one warning about the map on your phone.

  1. 1
    The main road

    Get to the Abraham Adesanya roundabout on the Lekki-Epe Expressway, in Ajah. By bus, ask for the Abraham Adesanya bus stop and get down there.

  2. 2
    Turn onto Ogombo Road

    Go through Ogombo Road toward Okun Ajah. From Abraham Adesanya it is about 7 km, around 25 minutes by car when the road is quiet. Go slowly on the last stretch: guests say it can be rough after rain.

    Barracuda Beach, Okun Ajah, through Ogombo Road
  3. 3
    Look for the painted wall

    The way in is lined with flags and leads to the painted BARRACUDA BEACH wall. That wall is the gate. The gate fee: message us and we reply with the fee for your day.

Questions and answers

What guests ask first.

QWhere exactly is the gate?

Follow the three steps in feature 01: the Abraham Adesanya roundabout, Ogombo Road, then the painted wall. If Google Maps stops you at a beach with another name, you are not at Barracuda. Send us your location and we will guide you.

QHow much is the gate fee?

Message us on WhatsApp and we reply with the fee for your day.

QCan I bring my own food and drinks?

Ask us when you message, and we tell you the rule.

QDo I pay on this website?

No. You send a request, we reply on WhatsApp and confirm the day. How you pay is agreed with the resort.

QIs there parking?

Free parking is listed on the resort's Google page.

QIs the beach crowded?

45 guests on Google say it is not crowded. Busy days can differ, so ask when you send your request.
