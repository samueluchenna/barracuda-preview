Before you arrive

Three steps from the main road.

  1. 1

    The main road

    Get to the Abraham Adesanya roundabout on the Lekki-Epe Expressway, in Ajah. By bus, ask for the Abraham Adesanya bus stop and get down there.

  2. 2

    Turn onto Ogombo Road

    Go through Ogombo Road toward Okun Ajah. From Abraham Adesanya it is about 7 km, around 25 minutes by car when the road is quiet.

    Barracuda Beach, Okun Ajah, through Ogombo Road
  3. 3

    Look for the painted wall

    The way in is lined with flags and leads to the painted BARRACUDA BEACH wall.

Google Maps can send you to another beach on this road. A beach with another name is not Barracuda. Go slowly on the last stretch: guests say it can be rough after rain.

Step 3, part one

Follow the flags.

The way in is lined with flags. If you can see them, keep going.

Step 3, part two

The painted wall is the gate.

If you are standing in front of this, you are at the right beach. The gate fee: message us and we reply with the fee for your day.
