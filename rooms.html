How to get here

  1. 1

    The main road

    Get to the Abraham Adesanya roundabout on the Lekki-Epe Expressway, in Ajah. By bus, ask for the Abraham Adesanya bus stop and get down there.

  2. 2

    Turn onto Ogombo Road

    Go through Ogombo Road toward Okun Ajah. From Abraham Adesanya it is about 7 km, around 25 minutes by car when the road is quiet.

    Barracuda Beach, Okun Ajah, through Ogombo Road

    Go slowly on the last stretch: guests say it can be rough after rain.

  3. 3

    Look for the painted wall

    The way in is lined with flags and leads to the painted BARRACUDA BEACH wall.
