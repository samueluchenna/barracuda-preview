How to find us

Three steps from the main road

Google Maps can send you to another beach on the same road. Follow these steps instead.

  1. 1

    The main road

    Get to the Abraham Adesanya roundabout on the Lekki-Epe Expressway, in Ajah. By bus, ask for the Abraham Adesanya bus stop and get down there.

  2. 2

    Turn onto Ogombo Road

    Go through Ogombo Road toward Okun Ajah. From Abraham Adesanya it is about 7 km, around 25 minutes by car when the road is quiet (Google Maps, Sunday 25 October).

    Barracuda Beach, Okun Ajah, through Ogombo Road

    Guests on Google say the road to the beach is rough: one says it is not tarred and a higher car is better, another says to avoid going when it rains. The resort will confirm. To confirm

  3. 3

    Look for the painted sign

    The painted BARRACUDA BEACH sign is the one guests look for. The clear landmark just before the gate goes here. To confirm

The Google Maps pin for Barracuda sits on the beachfront beside Eco Beach Resort and Westside Beach Resort. It is being corrected. If Maps stops you at a beach with another name, you are not at Barracuda: call 0708 865 4508.

Before you come

Where exactly is the gate?

Follow the three steps under "How to find us": Abraham Adesanya, then Ogombo Road, then the painted sign. Do not follow the Google Maps pin on its own, because it is being corrected and may stop at another beach.

How much is the gate fee?

The exact amount will be written here by the resort, so there is no argument at the gate. To confirm

Can I bring my own food and drinks?

Do I pay on this website?

No. You send a request, the resort replies on WhatsApp and confirms the day. How you pay is agreed with them. To confirm

What time is check-in and check-out?

Check-in is from 12:30pm and check-out is by 12:00 noon, as listed on the resort's Google page.
