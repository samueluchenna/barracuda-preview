# Barracuda Beach Resort: website preview (draft)

Version 4 (bright, new type, rooms page opens on a room). A first-look draft of a website for Barracuda Beach Resort, Okun Ajah, Lagos, built for the resort's General Manager. It is a private-link preview, not the resort's real site. Every page has `noindex, nofollow` and `robots.txt` disallows everything.

**Pictures are placeholders.** They are visitors' photos from the resort's Google Maps page (the round logo is cut from the resort's own Facebook poster). They are not the resort's own professional photos, and they will be replaced or credited before a real launch. The guest reviews are quoted word for word from the resort's Google page (read 28 October 2026), with the reviewers' names as shown there.

**Facts that are not confirmed show a dashed "To confirm" tag:** the gate fee, prices, house rules, gate hours and the exact gate pin. Nothing here is a price list.

Fonts: Unbounded (headlines) and Hanken Grotesk (text), both under the SIL Open Font License, self-hosted in `assets/fonts/`.

Built by Samuel Uchenna. To take it down: `gh repo delete samueluchenna/barracuda-preview`, or turn off Pages in the repository settings.
